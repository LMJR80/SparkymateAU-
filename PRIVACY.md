# Privacy Policy

SparkymateAU is a mobile wrapper for the SparkymateAU web application.

This app may collect or process information in the following ways:

- Usage data required to operate the web app
- Device or browser information needed for app stability and diagnostics
- User-entered content submitted through the app's tools and AI features

We do not sell personal data.

This policy should be reviewed and updated before publishing to the Play Store. Add your final privacy policy to a public web page and link it from the Play Store listing.
